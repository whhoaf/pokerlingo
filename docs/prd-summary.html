Poker Skill offers free lessons, a daily puzzle and coached games against AI, per its makers' own comparison post, which also lists GTO Poker Trainer: Preflop at $69.99 a year. 04 How one question works

1A fresh hand is dealt: new cards, pot and bet
↓
2You tap an answer or type a number
↓
3The app grades it and shows why
↓
4The scheduler sets the next review date
You never grade yourself. Right counts as Good, wrong as Again.
Why?

A rival trainer's write-up names the two ways flashcards fail at poker: flipping a card lets you recognise a chart instead of rebuilding it, and self-grading lets you over-rate half-right answers (Forge). The FSRS tutorial says grading with only Again and Good works fine, and its authors found it slightly more accurate (FSRS tutorial). Scheduling uses ts-fsrs; hands are evaluated with pokersolver. Both are MIT licensed.

06 Where your progress lives

↓

Why install first?

Safari deletes a site's stored data after 7 days of Safari use without a visit. Home Screen web apps are exempt, and their data is kept separate from Safari, so progress made in a Safari tab would not carry over (WebKit).
