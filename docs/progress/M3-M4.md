## What was built

**Content layer** (`src/content`, `content/`)

- `content/house-chart.json` and `content/sizing-rules.json`: placeholders. `"draft": true`, only the `TBD` source, all 13 situation IDs and all four rules (D2 to D5) present, plausible values.
- `types.ts`: the file shapes, the five actions and their button labels, the situation IDs.
- `validate.ts`: `validateHouseChart` and `validateSizingRules` return a list of problems in plain words. `assertHouseChart` and `assertSizingRules` throw on any. Rules checked: the plan's list (a source on every item, ranges parse, no hand in two action buckets except through `either`, every `either` accepts two or more of the situation's choices, positive numbers, `min` at most `max`, `TBD` and unknown source IDs) plus the extras under "Deviations".
- `chart.ts`: reads a situation. `lookupFor(situation).verdict(handClass)` gives the accepted actions, the colour action and whether the hand is borderline. `spotShapes` lists every table size and seat a situation can come up at.
- `sizing.ts`: turns a rule into the numbers a question accepts (chips for D2, D3 and D5, the pot fractions inside a range for D4) and writes ranges as text.
- `index.ts`: imports the two JSON files and turns sources into feedback lines and further-reading links.

**Engine** (`src/engine`)

- `ranges.ts`: the 169 hand classes, the range notation (`77`, `77+`, `22-66`, `AKs`, `AKo`, `AK`, `ATs+`, `A2s-A5s`), `combos`, grid cells, dealing real cards for a class, a fixed strength order for sampling and `nearEdge`.
- `seats.ts`: `seatNames(players)`, `chartSeat(seat, players)`, `seatMatches`, seats before you, seats counted back from the button.
- `texture.ts`: `classifyFlop` gives dry, wet or neither, with a plain-words reason.
- `types.ts` gained: `TableView.ring` (the seat picture), `GradeResult.rangeGrid`, `sources`, `draft` and `correctIds`, `SkillDef.draft` and a grid and sources on the worked example.

**Skills** (`src/skills/A`, `src/skills/D`)

- A1 names the seats: a ring of 6 to 9 seats, only the dealer button and one highlighted seat shown, four seat names to pick from.
- A2 to A8 share one generator (`situationSkill.ts`): pick a situation of the skill, a table size and seat the content allows, limpers or the raiser where the situation has them, and a hand (about 60% from near the edge of the listed ranges, the rest across all 169 classes by combinations). The grader accepts exactly the chart's actions for that hand.
- A9: half the questions ask which seat at a 9-player table the first seat at 6 players plays like (LJ, HJ, UTG, CO). Half show a hand whose accepted actions differ between `open.EP` and `open.LJ`, at a 6-player table. With content that has no such hand, every question is the seat question.
- D2: blinds 1 and 2, 0 to 3 limpers, raise to how many chips. D3: an open, you re-raise in position (button against cutoff) or out of position (a blind against the button). D4: a clearly dry or wet flop, six pot-fraction choices, every choice inside the rule is right. D5: river, strong made hand (straight or better using a hole card), the opponent calls too much.
- Every explainer and worked example is built at load time from the content files (ranges, either hands, sizes, reasons, sources). A worked example is a real question graded by the same code as the questions.
- Further reading: A1 to A9 link PokerCoaching and EV Poker. D2 to D5 link the cited sources that have a web address, so with the placeholder they show none.

**Components** (`src/app`)

- `SeatRing`: the table seen from above, seats round an oval, the highlighted seat at the bottom, a D chip on the button, you in gold, limpers in blue, the raiser in red, seats that folded dimmed, a text tag under marked seats. It has a screen-reader sentence.
- `RangeGrid`: the 13 by 13 chart in feedback and in worked examples. One solid colour per action, a corner dot for borderline hands, your hand outlined, a legend, and a sentence for screen readers. No stripes.
- `DraftNote`: "Draft advice" box, shown on explainers and feedback while the content file is a draft.
- Feedback shows the grid after the reason, then the working, then the sources. `ChoiceInput` takes a list of right choices, so an either hand or several right sizes all turn green.
- Skill map: the "coming in a later update" lines are gone. Home welcome text mentions seats and bet sizes.

## How the content files are read

Both files are imported at build time (`src/content/index.ts`). Skills are made by factories that take the content, for example `makeA2(chart)` and `makeD3(rules)`. The shipped skills are `makeA2(houseChart)` and so on. This lets the tests build the same skills from fixture content.

- A question reads: the situation's `seats`, `players`, `limpers`, `raiser`, `choices`, `ranges`, `either` and `default` (through `lookupFor`), its `reason` and its `sources`.
- Seats in a list match by row: `LJ` covers the first seat at 6 players, `UTG`, `UTG+1` and `UTG+2` cover only themselves and only where they are early seats.
- A hand in an `either` entry accepts that entry's actions. Otherwise it takes the action whose range lists it. Otherwise `default`.
- The grid colours a borderline hand with the first action of its `accept` list.
- Explainers quote the range strings as written in the file, for example "Raise: 77+, ATs+." and "Either is fine for 66, A9s (Raise or fold)."
- Sizing: D2 is `(base + perLimper x limpers)` big blinds times 2 chips, each end rounded to a whole chip. D3 and D5 are the multiplier times the open or the pot, each end rounded. D4 accepts a choice when it lies inside the range, give or take 0.01, so a written 0.33 or 0.67 matches 1/3 and 2/3.
- Number answers for D2, D3 and D5 must be whole chips.
- To change advice, edit the two JSON files only. `npm test` then checks the files and every skill built from them.

## Deviations from the plan

1. **Draft note scope.** The plan says every explainer and feedback screen for Areas A and D. A1 (naming seats) and D1 (arithmetic) teach no advice from a content file, so they do not show the note. A2 to A9 and D2 to D5 do. The A9 seat question has no chart content either, but it leads into the chart rows, so it shows the note too.
2. **Seat matching.** The plan matches seats by name or chart row. An early seat name listed in a file (for example `UTG`) only matches where that seat plays the early row, so it does not also match the first seat at 6 players, which plays the lojack row. Row names (`LJ`, `HJ`, `CO`, `BTN`, `SB`, `BB`) match every seat that uses the row.
3. **Range notation is a small superset.** Besides the plan's forms it accepts a run with the same gap (`T9s-54s`) and runs written in either order (`66-22`, `A5s-A2s`). Everything else throws.
4. **Validator extras.** All 13 situations and 4 rules must be present, no others. Every situation must be askable at some table (a seat, table size, limper count and raiser that fit). Each D4 range must contain at least one of the six choices. `perLimper` must be above zero (the plan says numbers are positive). A `TBD` source defined in a file whose `draft` is false is an error.
5. **Answers in chips are whole numbers** for D2, D3 and D5, and both ends of each accepted span are rounded to the nearest chip. The plan says "rounded to whole chips" without saying how.
6. **Choice contract.** `GradeResult.correctIds` (new) lets a question name several right buttons. The plan's contract has one `correctLabel` only, which cannot light up two buttons.
7. **D5 strong hand** is a straight or better made with at least one hole card. D3 shows opens of 5 to 12 chips. D4 pots are 15 to 120 in steps of 5, D5 pots 20 to 400. D3 position: in position is the button against the cutoff, out of position is a blind against the button, as the plan's examples say.
8. **Sampling order.** The plan says a "fixed strength order" without defining it. It is the Chen formula, with ties broken by high card, low card and suited first. It is used only to pick which hands to ask about, never to grade. "Near the edge" means within 10 places of the weakest hand of a listed range, plus every `either` hand.
9. **Intro order** is my choice (plan: "after the first few B and C skills"). Listed above.
10. **Welcome text** on Home now mentions seats and bet sizes. The screenshot check for "read the board" still holds.
11. **No PRD and plan conflicts** found. One thing to know: the PRD calls A6 "raise or fold", but the small blind and big blind rows of the placeholder chart also use call and check, as the plan's choices list allows.
