## Fix list

Items a to g are the ones named in the brief. Items h to k came from the exploratory pass.

**a. The Check button looked see-through before an answer.** It used `opacity: 0.5`. Now `.btn:disabled` is a solid `--dark` panel with a 2 px `--dark-2` edge, the hard shadow in `--sheet`, and fully opaque `--light` pixel text (6.1 to 1) with no text shadow. A test reads the stylesheet and fails if the disabled rule uses opacity or a translucent background.

**b. The gold note on the felt.** It was gold text straight on the translucent felt over the swirl. Now it sits on a solid `--sheet` pill: gold on `--sheet` is 7.7 to 1 whatever the swirl does. A style test computes the ratio from the tokens and requires at least 4.5.

**c. Explainers with long ranges.** Each Area A explainer (A2 to A9) now shows one block per situation, built by `situationBlock` in `src/skills/A/common.ts`: the title, where it applies, the reason as one short paragraph, the range text only when short, and the 13 by 13 `RangeGrid` with its key and no outlined hand. The old single paragraph is gone, so A6 is four blocks. The rule: a situation prints its range text when all its range strings together (action ranges plus either lists) add up to 60 characters or fewer (`SHORT_RANGE_CHARS`). With the shipped chart that prints it for the early seats and the lojack, and leaves it out for every other situation. The grid carries those. The body keeps three paragraphs: the intro, the notation and how to read the grid. Tests cover the exact boundary (60 printed, 61 not), a 169 hand range, every shipped situation, and that no long string leaks into any text.

**d. The feedback sheet for A2 to A8.** The sheet may be 90% of the screen high (was 82%). In Area A the chart is smaller (about 256 px wide) with its key beside it instead of below, and its title is dropped (it is in the screen reader label). Measured with `scripts/layout_check.py` on 390 by 844 over 20 to 30 random questions per skill: the result, the answer, the reason, your hand on the grid and the key are inside the visible part of the sheet every time. The working and sources scroll below. Next stays pinned and ends above the bottom edge.

**e. A1 kept "?" after the answer.** `Table` now takes `reveal`, and `Session`, `Practice` and the explainer example pass the answer. `revealFocus` fills the name of the highlighted seat only when it is hidden. Tested, and checked in the screenshot run (the highlighted seat shows the answer in feedback).

**f. The seat picture was too tall.** Three changes. (1) With a seat picture and no board, your two cards now lie on the table oval at board size, behind the seats, instead of in their own row with a label. (2) The table note moved into the oval when there are no cards (A1, A9), and sits below the picture otherwise. (3) Four short choices (A1, A9) sit two by two. On 390 by 844 every A question with its choices (A7 has three) ends above the bottom edge with no scroll. The tightest case is A8 at 841 px of 844 when its prompt wraps to four lines. Tap targets are unchanged: choices are 64 px.

**g. Raise size on the seat picture.** `SpotSeats.raiseTo` puts the size on the raiser's seat: D3 shows, for example, "5 chips" on the red seat, matching the prompt. A7 and A8 have no raise size in the chart, so they still say "Raise". The screen reader label says "BTN: raised to 5 chips." A screenshot check compares the label with the prompt.

**h. Seat labels overlapped other seats (found by the new layout check).** Under the old picture, a tag such as "Limp" hung below its seat and landed on the next seat in about one question in ten, and the dealer button chip sat on the small blind's seat in some A3 questions. The layout check tests every pair of seat pieces for overlap on every question and sheet it opens and finds none.

**i. Folded seats** were drawn with `opacity: 0.45`, which let your cards show through them. They are now a solid darker pill with light text (7.8 to 1).

**j. Choice labels in tags on blue and red** had poor contrast as plain white text. The dark label fixes it.

## Advice that reads oddly (content files, not edited)

1. `open.BTN` reason: "Play 3 to 5 times as many hands here as from the first seat." The chart opens roughly 400 combinations on the button against about 66 from the early seats, which is nearer 6 times (more with the either hands).
2. `limpers.early` reason: "The more players limp, the tighter you raise." The chart has one raise range whatever the number of limpers, so the question cannot grade that.
3. `limpers.SB` calls with hands such as 92s, 72s and K2o (the range runs to hundreds of characters). The reason says "almost anything suited, connected or ten high and better", which matches, but it may be wider than the owner expects.
4. D3 reason says "about 3 times" and "about 4 times". The rule accepts 3 to 4 times in position and 4 to 5 times out of position. A reader may think 5 times is wrong.
5. D4 reason says "a quarter to a third" and "two thirds to three quarters". The rule accepts 0.25 to 0.35 and 0.55 to 0.8 of the pot, so 1/4 and 1/3 are right on a dry board and 2/3 and 3/4 on a wet one, as written. It says nothing about 1/2, which is wrong on both.
6. `open.SB` has the same ranges as `open.BTN`, as its reason says. Worth confirming that this is meant.
