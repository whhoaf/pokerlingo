## Variant: Readable arcade

### Design stance
Balatro's look for everything you glance at, a normal font for everything you read.

### Key choices
- Typography: m6x11plus for numbers, labels, card ranks, buttons and results. The system font (San Francisco on iPhone) for questions, explanations and working.
- Colour: the same Balatro palette as Full arcade.
- Background: the same swirl, slower and lower contrast.
- Surfaces: no scanlines, rounder corners, the same hard offset shadows.
- Interaction: the same deal-in, button press, feedback slide and +1 pop. No idle wobble.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: long explanations and worked examples stay easy to read.
- Weak at: less of the CRT atmosphere.

### Best for
- A learning app where every question ends in a short explanation.
